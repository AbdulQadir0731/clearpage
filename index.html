Blocking beyond Safari

On iOS 26, iPadOS 26 and macOS 26, Clearpage Pro extends blocking to apps, games and other browsers using Apple’s URL filter.

  • Not a VPN. Your traffic never passes through Clearpage.
  • Private by design. Addresses are checked with Private Information Retrieval through an Apple relay, so neither Clearpage nor Apple learns which sites you visit.
  • Plays nicely. Works alongside VPNs, iCloud Private Relay and custom DNS.

Not available on Apple Vision Pro.

No data collection

There are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising identifiers in Clearpage. Safari applies the blocklists itself and never tells Clearpage which websites you visit. See the privacy policy for exactly what does and doesn’t leave your device.

Independent

No investors, and no “acceptable ads” programme. Nobody can pay to have their ads let through. Clearpage’s blocklists are built from well-known community filter lists, credited under Acknowledgements in the app.
