What Clearpage does

Clearpage supplies blocking rules to Safari and, with Clearpage Pro on iOS 26, iPadOS 26 and macOS 26, to the system-wide URL filter. It removes advertising, tracking, cookie notices and similar content from websites.

What it doesn’t promise

Clearpage’s rules are built from community-maintained filter lists. Websites change constantly, and advertising networks actively work around blockers. Clearpage does not guarantee that every advertisement, tracker or notice will be blocked, or that every website will work correctly while blocking is on.

If a website breaks, you can turn individual extensions off, or turn off blocking for that site in Safari. If something gets through, tell us with Report a Problem in the app.

Clearpage Pro

Ad blocking in Safari is free. Clearpage Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription that unlocks tracker blocking, cookie-notice and annoyance removal, regional lists, Clearpage Advanced and all-apps blocking.

  • Price: $0.99 per month, or $6.99 per year. Prices in other currencies are set by Apple for your region and shown in the app before you buy.
  • Free trial: the yearly plan includes a one-week free trial for subscribers who have not had one before. The monthly plan does not include a trial.
  • Billing: payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Renewal: the subscription renews automatically unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the period.
  • Managing it: manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on iPhone and iPad, or in the App Store on a Mac. Cancelling stops future renewals; the current period runs to its end.

If your subscription ends, free ad blocking in Safari keeps working; the Pro features stop.
